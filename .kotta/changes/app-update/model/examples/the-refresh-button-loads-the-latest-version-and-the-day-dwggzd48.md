# The refresh button loads the latest version and the day

## Given

The app on the home screen shows 1,240 kcal; a minute ago the person logged a 450 kcal lunch through Claude.

## When

The person taps the refresh button in the header.

## Then

The app loads again, and the plate shows 1,690 kcal with the lunch among today's entries.
