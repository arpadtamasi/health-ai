# A new version is out and the app was opened from the home screen

## Given

The app on the home screen was loaded at 8:00, and a new version was published at 12:00.

## When

The person brings the app back to the front at 13:00.

## Then

A bar above the plate says "Új verzió érhető el." with a "Frissítés" button; the plate, the line and the entries stay as they were. Tapping "Frissítés" loads the new version, and the plate shows the day as it is now.
