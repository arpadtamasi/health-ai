# Each third of the plate fills with its own food, one portion per entry

## Rule

The plate SHALL be divided into three equal thirds for protein, carbs and fat, each standing for that macro's daily goal and each filled with grains of its own food: red lentils for protein, rice for carbs, pumpkin seeds for fat. The share of a third covered with grains SHALL equal what was eaten against the goal, growing from the middle of the third outwards, and each logged entry SHALL add its own grains in a slightly different shade. A dashed contour SHALL mark how far the grains should reach by now. The glass SHALL show water filling from the bottom with a layer per glass and a dashed line for the plan. The centre of the plate SHALL show the calories eaten against the calorie goal.

## Rationale

The owner kept the plate ("A tányér maga jó") and chose grains, a different one for each macro ("Tetszik a rizs, de lehetne különböző a három"). Countable grains read more exactly than a filled area, and a different shape for each food keeps the thirds apart without relying on colour alone.

## Scope

The plate and the glass of the home. Equal thirds stay even when the goals differ in grams. Above a goal, see "Going over a goal is shown, never praised".

## Open decisions

- Which plate should the grains sit on: the dark speckled stoneware, where all three natural colours stand out and look the same in light and dark mode, or the light ceramic of today, where the rice needs a thin brown edge? (The assistant suggests the dark stoneware.)
