# In the evening the line says what still fits

## Rule

From the evening hour until the end of the eating window, when the calories are not over the goal, the line SHALL say how many kcal still fit today and how much of it is protein, instead of how far under the plan the person is. After the end of the eating window, the "What should I eat now?" button SHALL NOT be shown.

## Rationale

A linear plan says "700 kcal under the plan" at 21:40, which invites a large late meal; what still fits, and a quiet button after the window, say the same honestly without pushing.

## Scope

The line under the plate and the button on the home. The plan itself stays linear.

## Open decisions

- From what hour should the evening wording start? (The assistant suggests 19:00, three hours before the end of a 7:00 to 22:00 window; it could also be relative to the window's end.)
