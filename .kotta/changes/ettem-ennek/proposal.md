# Ettem, ennék

## Why

"nem az egyetlen use case a mit ehetek, ugyanolyan fontos a mit ettem" and "ha beszélgetésbe/fotóba kerülök, az lenne
a jó, ha kb ugyanaz az élmény lenne, mint a claude/chatgpt" (SZ1). The home gave "What should I eat now?" the one
prominent button while logging lived in an unlabelled text field; the conversation answered in one block, could not
take a photo from the gallery, forgot the photo after one step, lost a failed message and kept what it logged only in a
six-second bar.

## What changes

- The dock gets a switch, "Ettem · Ennék" (labels to be decided), which says whether a message or photo is what was
  eaten or a request for options; the "What should I eat now?" button under the plate goes (J1). Touches "Ask what to
  eat to catch up", "Start logging from one button on the home", "Open on a full-screen home that shows the day",
  "In the evening the line says what still fits", "After the eating window What should I eat now is gone", "The home
  shows the day's calories…" and "A photo logs what it shows".
- The conversation reads like a chat app: replies without a bubble and formatted, a logged entry stays as a card with
  Edit and Undo, a failed message offers "Újra", a photo is seen for two more messages, and the camera offers the
  gallery (J2, items 1–4): rule "The conversation keeps what happened in it".

Not in this change: replies streamed word by word with visible steps and Stop (J2, item 5, the next change), and memory
of habits and of what is at home (J3: later, to be thought through). Preview: the "Ettem, ennék, emlékszem" artifact.

## Open decisions

- The switch's labels: "Ettem · Ennék", "Beírom · Ajánlj" or "Napló · Ötlet".
