# The answer starts to show at once

## Source

A person who sends a message or a photo in the app.

## Stimulus

The message is sent.

## Environment

A phone on a mobile connection; the service may be starting after a quiet hour (no warm instance, J1).

## Artifact

The conversation sheet and the "What should I eat now?" sheet.

## Response

The person's message SHALL show at once, a first sign of the answer (a step or the first words) SHALL follow without waiting for the whole reply, and the reply SHALL keep growing until it is done or stopped.

## Measure

To be set by the owner: how many seconds after Send the first step or word shows on a warm service, and after a quiet hour.

## Open decisions

- How soon must the first step or word show after Send on a warm service, and how soon after a quiet hour?
