# The home has no row of figures

## Why

"És redundáns az adat. Kellenek a tápanyag chipek a rajz alá?" (SZ1). The row of figures repeated the plate, the line and the
legend; the owner chose A (J1): drop the row, keep the grams one tap away in the centre of the plate, give the glass its litres.

## What changes

- The home shows no row of figures; a tapped third shows its grams in the centre of the plate; the glass shows the water drunk
  and its goal in litres under it. Rule "Each macro has its own colour, and words state the situation".
- The story "Choose what the home shows" and the examples that tapped or read the row now use the plate; the last known day is
  drawn with the glass's litres.
- New example "The glass shows its litres under it".
