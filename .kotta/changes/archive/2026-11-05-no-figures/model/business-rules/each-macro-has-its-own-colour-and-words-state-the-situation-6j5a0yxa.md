# Each macro has its own colour, and words state the situation

## Rule

Protein, carbs, fat and water SHALL each be drawn in the colour of its food wherever they appear on the home (the plate, the glass, the legend and the dots of the entries): coral for protein (red lentils), cream for carbs (rice), olive for fat (pumpkin seeds), blue for water; and no element SHALL change colour to state how the day is going. The situation SHALL be stated by the line under the plate, in words and with a drawn mark, and a lag SHALL be visible as the gap between the grains and the dashed mark of where the plan stands now. The home SHALL NOT show a row of figures under the plate: the grams of a macro SHALL be shown in the centre of the plate when its third is tapped, and the glass SHALL show the water drunk and its goal in litres under it. The line SHALL say the calories are under the plan only when they are more than 120 kcal under the plan for this time of day.

## Rationale

The row of figures repeated what the plate, the line and the legend already say ("És redundáns az adat"); the owner chose to drop it, keep the grams one tap away and give the glass its litres (no-figures J1). With one colour per state, the water turned green and the hero red for most of the day; the owner chose colours that name the macro. Taking the colours from the food itself ("A színeket gondold át") makes the plate read as food, and the shapes keep the thirds apart for colour-blind eyes too.

## Scope

The home of the app. Before 9:00 the line does not judge the pace, because the plan is near zero. Over a goal and in the evening, see the two rules on those.
