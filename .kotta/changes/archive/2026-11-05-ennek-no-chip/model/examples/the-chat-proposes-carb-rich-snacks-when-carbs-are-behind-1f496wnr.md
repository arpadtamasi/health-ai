# What to eat proposes carb-rich snacks when carbs are behind

## Given

It is 14:20, carbs are 50 g behind the plan for now and calories are 209 kcal under pace.

## When

The user switches the dock to "Ennék" and types what they can choose from.

## Then

The sheet says the user is 50 g behind on carbs and offers snacks with about 60 g of carbs, each with its calories (oat bran with soy yoghurt and a banana, 65 g and 320 kcal; two slices of wholegrain bread with jam, 58 g and 290 kcal), and the chosen one is previewed on the plate.
