# Half of the protein goal fills half of the protein third

## Given

The protein goal is 160 g. Breakfast had 17 g, the eggs 13 g, lunch 38 g and a curd 12 g, 80 g in all.

## When

The person looks at the plate.

## Then

The protein third is filled over half of its area in four layers, one per entry, and its edge stands at about 71 percent of the radius.
