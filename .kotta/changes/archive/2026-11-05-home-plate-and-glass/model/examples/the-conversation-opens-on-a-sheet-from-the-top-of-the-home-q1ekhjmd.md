# The conversation opens on a sheet from the top of the home

## Given

The person wrote "két tojás" this morning and the assistant logged two boiled eggs.

## When

They tap the conversation button at the top of the home.

## Then

A sheet shows today's conversation in order, and closing it brings back the plate; the home itself shows no thread.
