# Each macro has its own colour, and words state the situation

## Rule

Protein, carbs, fat and water SHALL each be drawn in a colour of their own wherever they appear on the home (the plate, the glass, the row of figures and the dots of the entries), and no element SHALL change colour to state how the day is going. The situation SHALL be stated by the line under the plate, in words and with a drawn mark (under the plan, on the plan, ahead of the plan, done), and a lag SHALL be shown as hatching between what was eaten and where the plan stands now. The line SHALL say the calories are under the plan only when they are more than 120 kcal under the plan for this time of day.

## Rationale

With one colour per state, the water turned green in the glass and the whole hero turned red for most of the day; the owner chose colours that name the macro and words that state the situation (J1 answer 3, J2 answer 2).

## Scope

The home of the app. Water is blue. Before 9:00 the line does not judge the pace, because the plan is near zero.
