# The plate fills each third by area, one layer per entry

## Rule

The plate SHALL be divided into three equal thirds for protein, carbs and fat, each standing for that macro's daily goal. The filled area of a third SHALL be proportional to what was eaten against the goal, and each logged entry SHALL add its own layer, outwards from the centre. A dashed arc SHALL mark where the plan stands now. The fat third SHALL treat its goal as a cap: its rim is drawn thick, and what is eaten above the cap SHALL spill onto the rim of the plate. The glass SHALL show water the same way, filling from the bottom with a layer per glass and a dashed line for the plan. The centre of the plate SHALL show the calories eaten against the calorie goal.

## Rationale

The owner asked for a plate cut in thirds that fills as they eat (SZ1). Filling by area keeps half a goal looking like half; filling by radius would make half look like three quarters.

## Scope

The plate and the glass of the home. Equal thirds stay even when the goals differ in grams; for the owner's goals the calorie shares are about 30, 35 and 34 percent.
