# Choose what the home shows

## Story

As a phone user, I want to tap a third of the plate, the glass, or a figure in the row and have the line under the plate and the centre of the plate show that instead of the calories (and tap it again, or the centre, to go back), so that I can follow the number I care about today.

## Value

The owner's question: "válthatom, hogy melyiket mutatja? ráklikkelek a fehérjére és az kerül oda?" The calories are the default, but today's concern is often protein or fat.

## Notes

For a minimum (protein, carbs, water) the line says how far behind or ahead of the plan the person is; for fat it says how much of the cap is left or how far it is over. The chosen third or glass is outlined, the others fade, and today's entries show their amount of it. The choice lasts for the session; the next day starts on the calories.
