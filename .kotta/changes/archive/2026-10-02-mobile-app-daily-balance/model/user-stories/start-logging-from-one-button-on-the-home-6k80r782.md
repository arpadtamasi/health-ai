# Start logging from one button on the home

## Story

As a phone user, I want one Log button on the Status, with one or two of my most frequent actions beside it (for example a glass of water, or the camera), so that what I do five times a day is really one tap.

## Value

Logging is the most frequent action; it should not need a screen change first.

## Notes

Screenshot: ![Status with the Log button and two frequent buttons](screens/home-20-status.png)

The Log button opens the Chat, where the quick buttons (frequent foods, water, the camera) and the composer are. The frequent buttons beside it act at once: the water button logs the usual glass, the camera button opens the camera.

## Decided by the owner

- One Log button, and one or two frequent buttons beside it so that the most frequent action is a single tap ("igen, de 1-2 gyakori gomb ott lehetne a log mellett - hogy tényleg 1 klikk legyen amit naponta 5x nyomok (pl víz) vagy ha fotó, akkor az"). The frequent ones are the most used actions of the person (by use in the last 30 days), like the usual foods; the buttons come from the history, so a new person sees only the Log button and the camera at first.
