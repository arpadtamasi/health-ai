# Halving a logged burrito replaces the entry

## Given

A burrito of 315 kcal is logged for lunch.

## When

The user sets the portion to half and saves the correction.

## Then

The entry shows 158 kcal; in Google Health the old entry is gone and one new entry exists, so the meal list shows one burrito, not two.
