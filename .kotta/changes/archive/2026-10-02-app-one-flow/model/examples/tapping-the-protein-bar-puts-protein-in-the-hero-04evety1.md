# Tapping the protein bar puts protein in the hero

## Given

It is 17:40, the calories are 200 under the plan and protein is 90 g to go.

## When

The person taps the protein bar.

## Then

The hero says how far protein is behind the plan, the chart shows the protein plan and what was eaten, the bar is outlined, and the calories are one tap away.
