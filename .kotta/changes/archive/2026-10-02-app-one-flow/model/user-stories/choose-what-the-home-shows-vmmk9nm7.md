# Choose what the home shows

## Story

As a phone user, I want to tap the bar of protein, carbs, fat or water and have the hero and the chart show that instead of the calories (and tap it again to go back), so that I can follow the number I care about today.

## Value

The owner's question: "válthatom, hogy melyiket mutatja? ráklikkelek a fehérjére és az kerül oda?" The calories are the default, but today's concern is often protein or fat.

## Notes

For a minimum (protein, carbs, water) the hero says how far behind or ahead of the plan the person is and the chart has the plan diagonal; for fat it says how much of the cap is left and the chart shows the cap as a line. The chosen bar is outlined. The choice lasts for the session; the next day starts on the calories.
