# See where I am against the day's pace

## Story

As a phone user, I want the top of the home to be a plate cut into thirds for protein, carbs and fat, each filling with grains of its own food as I log, and a glass for water, with a mark where the plan stands now, so that I see at a glance what I am short of.

## Value

The first view answers "what am I behind on right now". The owner's example: "ma épp szénhidráttal vagyok lemaradva".

## Notes

See "Each third of the plate fills with its own food, one portion per entry" and "Each macro has its own colour, and words state the situation". The plan is the goal spread evenly over 7:00 to 22:00, as before. The chart of the day (plan diagonal and staircase) is gone: the plate's dashed contour and the glass's dashed line show the pace.

Carbs and protein goals start from a default split of the calorie goal, and the activity does not raise the day's allowance (both as decided earlier).
