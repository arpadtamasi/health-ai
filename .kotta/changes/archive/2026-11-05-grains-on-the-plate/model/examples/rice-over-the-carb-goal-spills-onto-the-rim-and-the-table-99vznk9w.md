# Rice over the carb goal spills onto the rim and the table

## Given

The carb goal is 185 g and 300 g were eaten.

## When

The person looks at the plate.

## Then

The carb third is full of rice, and the rice above the goal lies spilled on the rim of the plate and the table beside it, the more the further over.
