# Load the new version from the home-screen app

## Story

As a phone user who keeps the app on the home screen, I want the app to bring itself up to date when I come back to it, the newest version and the day as it is now, so that I get fixes and see what I logged through Claude without removing the app from the home screen or pressing anything.

## Value

The home-screen app has no address bar and no reload, so today a fix reaches the phone only by chance, and entries logged in Claude show up on the plate only after the day is loaded again.

## Notes

The app is installable with its own name ("Health AI") and the "three grains" icon (J3), and opens without the browser bar. The web app in a browser tab behaves the same. A button was asked for first (SZ1); the owner then chose loading by itself, with no button (J1, J2).
