# What was logged stays in the conversation as a card

## Given

In the conversation the assistant logged half a portion of ropa vieja with rice, 480 kcal.

## When

The person scrolls back to it an hour later, and taps Undo on its card.

## Then

The card is still in the conversation with the name, 480 kcal and the macros, with Edit and Undo; after Undo it says the entry was undone, and the plate drops by 480 kcal.
