# The first step shows within a second and a half

## Given

The service is running (a message was sent a minute ago) and the person sends a photo of their lunch on "Ettem".

## When

They tap Send.

## Then

Within 1.5 seconds the line "Megnézem a képet" shows under their message; the reply then grows under it. After a quiet hour the same line shows within 6 seconds, with the "the connection is slow" notice until then.
