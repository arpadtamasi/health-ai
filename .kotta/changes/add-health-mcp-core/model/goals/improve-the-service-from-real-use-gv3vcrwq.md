# Feedback comes from real use

## Outcome

Feedback about Health AI comes from real use, from users and from the AI agents themselves (for example when a task took too many calls), so the owner knows what to improve.

## Context

Owner and invited testers use the service through AI chat clients; the owner cannot watch those conversations directly.

## Baseline and target

Baseline: no feedback channel and no record of why tools are called.

Target: none. The owner decided on 2026-09-26 that this goal needs no numeric target.
