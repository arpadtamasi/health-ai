# Health AI user

## Role

The owner or an invited tester who wears a Fitbit and uses their own Google Health data from an AI chat.

## Goals

Ask about their own health data; log meals and water; run experiments later.

## Responsibilities

Signs in with their own allow-listed Google account; grants the Google Health scopes; reconnects when access expires; may delete their data.
