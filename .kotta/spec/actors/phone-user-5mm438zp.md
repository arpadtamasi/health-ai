# Phone user

## Role

The owner, and later invited testers, who wear a Fitbit and use Health AI from a phone instead of from a chat.

## Goals

Know at any moment of the day how much they can still eat; log food and water without typing a conversation.

## Responsibilities

Signs in with their own Google account; sets their own daily goal; checks the quantities the camera proposes before anything is logged.
