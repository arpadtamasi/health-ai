# The home shows the day's calories, carbs, protein and water as a full-screen picture

## Given

It is 14:20, the goal is 1,900 kcal over 7:00 to 22:00, 720 kcal were eaten, carbs are 50 g behind and 0.9 of 2.0 litres of water were drunk.

## When

The person opens the app.

## Then

The home fills the screen with the biggest gap (carbs, 50 g behind) as the main figure and calories, carbs, protein and water as large pictures against their plan for 14:20; the composer and the one logging button are visible without scrolling.
