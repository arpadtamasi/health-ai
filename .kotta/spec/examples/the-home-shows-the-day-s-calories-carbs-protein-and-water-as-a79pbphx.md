# The home shows the day as a pace chart and bars

## Given

It is 14:20, the goal is 2,100 kcal over 7:00 to 22:00 and 1,300 kcal were eaten with protein 90 g to go.

## When

The person opens the app.

## Then

The hero shows the calories against the plan, the chart shows the plan diagonal and the staircase of the meals, and the four bars show protein, carbs, fat and water; the dock and the text field are visible without scrolling.
