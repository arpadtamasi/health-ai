# A new version loads when the app comes back from the background

## Given

The app on the home screen was loaded at 8:00 and then put in the background; a new version was published at 12:00. Nothing is typed and no sheet is open.

## When

The person brings the app back to the front at 13:00.

## Then

The app loads the new version and shows the day as it is now. No bar and no button appear.
