# A lunch photo from the gallery is logged afterwards

## Given

The person took a photo of their lunch at 13:10 with the phone's camera and did not log it. It is 15:00 and the dock is on "Ettem".

## When

They tap the camera button, choose the photo library in the phone's own menu and pick the lunch photo.

## Then

The lunch is logged as an estimate with a card that offers Edit and Undo; its time can be changed to 13:10 with Edit.
