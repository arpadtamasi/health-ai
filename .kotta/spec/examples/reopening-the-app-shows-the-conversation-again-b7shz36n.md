# Reopening the app shows the conversation again

## Given

The person wrote with the assistant this morning and a photo was part of it.

## When

They open the app on another phone the same afternoon.

## Then

The morning's conversation is back (their messages and the answers), the photo is shown as a note that a photo was sent, and no photo is kept on our side.
