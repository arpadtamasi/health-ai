# Tapping an entry highlights its layer on the plate

## Given

Five entries were logged today, among them lunch at 13:30.

## When

The person taps the lunch in the row of today's entries.

## Then

Lunch's layer stays strong in each third and the other layers fade; a second tap brings them back.
