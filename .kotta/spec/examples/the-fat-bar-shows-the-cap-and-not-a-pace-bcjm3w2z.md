# The fat third shows the cap

## Given

The fat goal is 80 g and 72 g were eaten.

## When

The person looks at the fat third and taps it.

## Then

The fat third is filled over 90 percent of its area inside its thick rim, the row reads 72 of 80 g, and the line says 8 g is left of the cap.
