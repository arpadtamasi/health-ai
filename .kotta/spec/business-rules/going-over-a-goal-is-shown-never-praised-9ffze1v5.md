# Going over a goal is shown, never praised

## Rule

When the calories are more than 50 kcal over the goal, the line SHALL say how far over ("Kereten felül: +635 kcal.") and which macros are over, with the warning mark, and SHALL NOT say the day is done or show a tick. When a macro is over its goal, the grains above the goal SHALL spill out of its third onto the rim of the plate and the table beside it; this holds for protein, carbs and fat alike, and fat is still described as a cap in words.

## Rationale

The owner is losing weight; a day 30 percent over the goal shown as done with a tick breaks trust in the numbers (the critique). The spill makes the excess visible in the same picture (E1: "Így a túlfolyás is jól megoldható.").

## Scope

The line under the plate and the plate. Water may go over its goal without a warning.

## Decided by the owner

- More than 50 kcal over the goal counts as over; within 50 kcal the day is done as before ("2b").
