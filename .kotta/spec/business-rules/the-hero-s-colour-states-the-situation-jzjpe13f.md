# The hero's colour states the situation

## Rule

The hero of the home SHALL be red only when the calories are more than 120 kcal under the plan for this time of day, yellow when the person is within 120 kcal of the plan or ahead, and dark once the day is closed; no other element of the home is red because of a state (red is also the brand colour of buttons and the logo).

## Rationale

A red hero all day, as first drawn, stops meaning anything and ends the day on a failure; the design critique found red doing three jobs at once.

## Scope

The hero of the home. Before 9:00 the hero does not judge the pace, because the plan is near zero.
