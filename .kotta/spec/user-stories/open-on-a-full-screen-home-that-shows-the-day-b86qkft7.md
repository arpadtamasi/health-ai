# Open on a full-screen home that shows the day

## Story

As a phone user, I want the app to be one screen: on top a plate and a glass that fill as I eat and drink, with the calories in the centre of the plate and one line under it that says where I stand; then a row with protein, carbs, fat and water; then today's entries; and at the bottom a dock with a switch between logging what I ate and asking what to eat, my usual items, the camera and the text field, so that I see where I stand and act on it without changing screens.

## Value

Logging and asking happen ten times a day. Longer conversations are comfortable in Claude already (SZ3), so the app is for a glance and a tap.

## Notes

Preview: the "Terített asztal" artifact of this change. The conversation with the assistant is on a sheet behind a button at the top; the answer to a quick log is a short bar above the dock. While the keyboard is open the plate folds into one line with a small plate, the state, the calories and the time. There is no large calorie figure and no chart of the day (SZ4, J1). Before the first log the plate is empty and the line says what the day's goals are.

This replaces the one thread of entries and conversation on the home (J2).
